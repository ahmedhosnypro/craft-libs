## Summary

PhotoCraft merged a generic, app-agnostic fix for a failure mode every wgpu
consumer in the suite shares: unwatched GPU work panicking in wgpu's default
error handler on a lost device. The containment scopes (validation,
internal-error, out-of-memory) are device-level, not app-domain.

## Evidence

- storytold/photocraft#4894 — "fix(gpu): contain errors in unwatched compositor
  work" (haranishi, merged 2026-10-13T17:24:38Z). 2 files, +222/−13:
  `crates/gpu/src/lib.rs`, `crates/gpu/tests/device_loss.rs`.
  Unwatched native `render` and `encode` calls are wrapped in validation,
  internal-error and out-of-memory scopes, returning `Unsupported` on a captured
  error; a destroyed-device fixture reproduces the invalid-buffer panic. Watched
  callers keep health callbacks; async WebGPU handling unchanged. Refs
  photocraft#1154, related #243; builds on the #3931 uniform-buffer preflight.

## Why this is generic, not app-domain

The diff contains no PhotoCraft document or layer model: it is wgpu error-scope
plumbing with a reusable destroyed-device test fixture. Every suite app that
renders through wgpu (the repo-list lists `gpu` as a candidate for photocraft,
filmcraft, effectcraft, lightcraft) needs the identical containment.

## Proposed extraction (for human decision)

A `craft-gpu` crate carrying:

- error-containment scope wrappers for native render/encode work;
- the destroyed-device panic-reproduction fixture as a shared test harness;
- an optional DeviceHealth callback boundary (watched-callers API).
